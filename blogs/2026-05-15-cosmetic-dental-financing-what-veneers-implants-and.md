Why Cosmetic Dental Treatment Is a Financing Conversation Cosmetic dental treatment is a financing conversation because insurance almost never pays for it. Veneers, cosmetic Invisalign, and most implant cases are classified as elective procedures, which means the full cost falls to the patient.
